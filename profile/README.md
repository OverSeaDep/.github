## 我们做什么 / What We Do

**市场研究与数字资产 / Market Research & Digital Assets**
面向目标市场的产业出海研究：行业扫描、竞争样本与需求传导分析、发展阶段对标。所有结论与数字按「已确认 / 需核实 / 低置信度」标注并关联来源，形成可持续更新、可追溯、可解释的研究资产。研究仓库（如全球 AI 市场分析等）以私有仓库维护，仅授权成员可见；公开仓库在下方单独列示。

Industry-level go-global research for target markets: market scans, competitor sampling, demand-transmission analysis, and development-stage benchmarking. Every conclusion and figure is tagged *confirmed / needs verification / low confidence* with linked sources, forming updatable, traceable, explainable research assets.

**AI 运营与客户触达 / AI-Assisted Operations & Customer Outreach**
AI 驱动的运营工作流：数字员工与自动化响应、批量内容与视频生产、广告文案与投放素材、多模型图像 / 视频生成与人工复核闸门。生成操作留痕、证据归档，只采纳经确认的结果。

AI-driven operations: digital-staff automation and response, batch content and video production, ad copy and media assets, multi-model image/video generation with human review gates. All operations are recorded as evidence and only confirmed results are adopted.

**本地化运营与验证 / Localized Operations & Validation**
线上获客 → 当地内容与渠道 → 线下验证 → 客户数据沉淀的闭环，让策略在真实市场中被检验而非停留在桌面推演。

A closed loop from online acquisition to local content and channels, on-the-ground validation, and customer-data accumulation — strategies are tested in real markets instead of staying on a desk.

**供应链协同与履约 / Supply-Chain Coordination & Fulfillment**
预售、拼柜与物流协同、商品资料与供应商报价管理、采购跟单与履约，把「能卖」与「能交付」衔接起来。

Presale, container consolidation and logistics coordination, product-intake and supplier-quotation management, purchasing follow-through, and fulfillment — connecting "can sell" with "can deliver".

**软件与工程化工具 / Software & Tooling**
自研商品资料与供应商报价平台、媒体批量生产工具链、AI Agent（Skill）工具箱与操作复盘 / 评审系统，沉淀可复用的内部能力。

In-house product-intake and supplier-quotation platform, media batch-production toolchains, AI-agent (skill) toolkits, and operation-recorder/review systems that turn repeatable work into reusable capability.

**客户服务模式 / Client Engagements**
「AI 海外增长启动」项目：模块化采购、里程碑推进、证据化验收。分阶段完成 诊断 → 决策 → 交付 → 验收 → 移交，范围、价格与交付证据以合同附件为准。

Our "AI overseas-growth kickoff" engagements are modular, milestone-based, and accepted against explicit evidence. Phases run diagnosis → decision → delivery → acceptance → handover, with scope, pricing, and delivery evidence defined in contract appendices.

## 工作原则 / How We Work

- **证据优先 / Evidence over claims** — 区分「已确认」「讨论假设」「待验证」；可发布的数字必须能追溯到来源。Facts are separated from assumptions, and published numbers must be traceable to sources.
- **不承诺做不到的事 / No overpromising** — 不以询盘、订单、销售额或回报率作为承诺，交付与验收边界白纸黑字。We never promise inquiries, orders, sales, or returns; delivery and acceptance boundaries are written down.
- **AI 辅助，人工负责 / AI-assisted, human-accountable** — AI 生成内容经过操作留痕、证据归档与评审闸门，最终判断由人作出。AI output is recorded, evidenced, and gated by review; humans make the final call.
- **安全与合规 / Security & compliance** — 凭据与客户数据不进入仓库；个人信息、未公开证据与内部信息按需授权访问。Credentials and customer data never enter repositories; personal data, unpublished evidence, and internal information are access-controlled on a need-to-know basis.
- **独立核验声明 / Independent verification** — 本组织的研究与文档用于决策支持，不构成投资、法律、税务或市场进入意见。Research here supports decisions but is not investment, legal, tax, or market-entry advice.
