# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

简体中文 | English

本准则适用于 OverSeaDep 组织下的全部公开与私有仓库、Issue、Pull Request、讨论与相关协作场景。具体仓库有更严格规定时，以更严格者为准。

This code applies to all public and private repositories, Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and related collaboration under the OverSeaDep organization. Where a repository has stricter rules, the stricter rule wins.

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

- **尊重与职业化**：基于事实与证据交流，不做人身攻击、不泄露他人隐私。
- **保密边界**：客户数据、未公开证据与内部信息只用于授权目的。
- **证据与诚信**：不虚构来源、实验结果、结论或项目状态；错误被发现时及时更正并记录。
- **安全合规**：不把凭据、敏感数据带入仓库，遵守适用的法律与平台规则。
- **AI 使用透明**：AI 辅助产出保留留痕，模型输出不冒充人工核验结论。

- **Respect & professionalism**: communicate with facts and evidence; no personal attacks, no disclosure of others' privacy.
- **Confidentiality**: customer data, unpublished evidence, and internal information are used only for authorized purposes.
- **Evidence & integrity**: never fabricate sources, experimental results, conclusions, or project status; correct and record errors when found.
- **Security & compliance**: keep credentials and sensitive data out of repositories and follow applicable laws and platform rules.
- **Transparent AI use**: keep traces of AI-assisted output; model output never masquerades as human-verified conclusions.

## 不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable Behavior

- 骚扰、歧视、威胁或任何形式的霸凌；
- 公开或未经授权传播客户数据、个人信息、凭据与未公开证据；
- 故意提交虚假数据、伪造来源或误导性结论；
- 规避访问控制或滥用组织权限。

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or bullying in any form;
- disclosing or unauthorized distribution of customer data, personal information, credentials, or unpublished evidence;
- knowingly submitting fabricated data, forged sources, or misleading conclusions;
- circumventing access controls or abusing organization permissions.

## 执行 / Enforcement

- 仓库所有者与组织管理员负责裁定与处理违规行为。
- 处理措施可包括：要求更正、移除内容、临时或永久移除成员/协作者资格。
- 严重的安全或法律问题（如凭据泄露、数据泄露）按 `SECURITY.md` 处理。

- Repository owners and organization admins are responsible for judging and handling violations.
- Consequences may include: required corrections, content removal, and temporary or permanent removal of member/collaborator status.
- Serious security or legal issues (e.g. credential or data leakage) are handled per `SECURITY.md`.
